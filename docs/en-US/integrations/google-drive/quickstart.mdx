Translate a Google Doc or Google Slides presentation without moving it out of Drive. The integration creates a separate copy for each language and keeps that copy up to date.

## Before you start [#before-start]

You need a [Project](/docs/platform/dashboard/get-started) and a Google account that can open the files you want to translate. If your team uses an Organization shared drive connection, you do not need to connect a personal account.

## Quickstart [#quickstart]

### 1. Connect Google Drive

In the Dashboard, open **Project > Integrations > Catalog**. Find **Google Drive**, click **Connect**, and choose how your Project connects:

- **Personal account:** click **Connect account**, then sign in to Google. The integration can access only the files you select and the translated copies it creates.
- **Organization shared drive:** select your team's existing connection.

<Callout type="info">
  **Organization admins:** Before teammates can choose a shared drive, open **Organization > Connections**, click **Add connection**, then **Set up** for Google Drive. Add the displayed email to the shared drive as a **Content manager**, return to the Dashboard, and click **Verify access**.
</Callout>

### 2. Add a file

Click **Add files**, then choose a Google Doc or Google Slides presentation. Check that the language in the **Source** column matches the file.

### 3. Choose languages

Select the file. Under **Translate into**, choose the languages you want.

If no languages are available yet, click **Select locales** to add them to the Project.

### 4. Translate

Click **Translate**. You can leave the page while translation continues.

When translation finishes, open the translated copy from the integration or from Google Drive. A file named `Launch plan` translated into French appears as `Launch plan [French]`.

To update that copy after the source changes, see [Managing translations](/docs/integrations/google-drive/guides/managing-translations).
