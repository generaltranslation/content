## About

General Translation is providing **up to $2,000 in translation credits** to eligible Slash customers. We put those credits toward localizing your app, website, documentation, and marketing content, so your product ships in every language you need.

We use Slash ourselves, so this is personal. If you bank with Slash and your customers are global (or you plan to grow internationally!), **we’d love to help translate your product into every language.**

## Why Slash and GT

[Slash](https://www.slash.com/) is building the financial command center for businesses. They process over [$30 billion](https://www.slash.com/blog/series-c) in yearly payment volume for 10,000+ companies. Customers frequently cite Slash’s **reliability, technical approach, and responsiveness**, which go far above and beyond traditional banking vendors.

We are happy Slash customers! We particularly love using the [Slash API](https://docs.slash.com/introduction), which allows us to build automations like a GitHub Action which creates approved vendor cards directly from PRs in our company repo.

Slash cares deeply about serving global businesses.

“Every business owner in the world needs a bank account,” [wrote](https://www.slash.com/blog/series-c) CEO Victor Cardenas in announcing Slash’s Series C. Slash supports sending and receiving [global payments](https://www.slash.com/platform/global-payments) to 180+ countries, and introduced [Slash Global Cards](https://www.slash.com/blog/now-live-global-usd-cards) in 2026 to allow international businesses to spend USD without a U.S. bank account.

We take a similar technical approach to product localization: making it easy and frictionless for companies to reach customers wherever they are and in whatever language they use.

We’re thrilled to work with Slash to further **our shared global mission** and to support ambitious companies to reach customers in every language.

## How GT works

Localization has traditionally meant wiring together separate platforms for code, content translations, and versioning. An internationalization library (i18n), Language Service Provider (LSP), and Translation Management System (TMS) each address only one piece of the problem and see only one fragment of context.

General Translation takes a different approach. **We’ve built the full stack for localization**: connecting code, content, translations, and review workflows.

Because we work across the full stack, we understand a product’s **full context and codebase**. Which simply means a better localization experience both for developers and for end users. We’re responsible for translations reflecting the logic of your application, staying up to date with new releases, syncing across every surface (marketing site, docs, and product), and staying true to your unique brand voice and terminology.

GT is trusted by world-class engineering teams at Cursor, Ramp, Sierra, Profound, Partiful, and Cognition. We’re excited to welcome Slash’s customers alongside them.

## How to use translation credits

For eligible Slash customers, translation credits can be applied to localize your **app, website, documentation, slides, design files**, and other supported content workflows.

To claim your $2,000 in credits, [sign up](https://dash.generaltranslation.com/signin) for GT. Fill out the [quick Slash form](https://generaltranslation.com/enterprise/contact/slash) on our page or redeem on [Slash’s website](https://www.slash.com/earn/perks).

*Translation credits are subject to General Translation’s review and approval.*

If you’d like to talk through your setup first, or if you’re a larger team thinking about enterprise deployment, [reach out](https://generaltranslation.com/enterprise/contact) to our team and we’re happy to set up a demo.

We’re looking forward to seeing where you launch next.
