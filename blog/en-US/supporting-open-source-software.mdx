## About

The open-source ethos is core to what we do at General Translation. Our flagship internationalization (i18n) libraries are open source under the MIT license.

<GitHub />

We’re now supporting other open-source developers who build and maintain software for the public good, starting with **$15,000 in no-strings-attached funds across fifteen developers**. Each will receive a $1,000 cash grant and $5,000 in General Translation credits.

## Who should apply

To be eligible, projects must:

- Meet the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd)
- Be actively developed or maintained (commits within the past year)
- Be able to receive funding through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors)

We’re looking to fund projects that many developers rely on but that lack institutional support. We’re also excited to fund creative and novel directions, and especially keen to see more projects in the language infrastructure and i18n space (although this is not a requirement).

## Apply or nominate a project

Reply to our grant announcement on X with a project link to apply or nominate another developer. If the post is not yet available, follow [@generaltxn](https://x.com/generaltxn) for the launch:

<Tweet id='1929971006585876517' />

Submissions and nominations will be accepted on a rolling basis. Payments will be made through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors), and General Translation credits will be granted directly. Grantee projects will also be featured on our blog.

We’re excited to see what you’re building!
