You have an app with 50,000 words of UI copy and you want to launch it in 17 languages: 850,000 words to translate on the first run. [CSA Research forecasts](https://www.mytotalretail.com/article/content-localization-as-the-key-to-success-in-global-markets/) that 17 languages will reach 90% of internet-accessible GDP by 2027. Here is what that costs with professional human translators, and what it costs using General Translation.

## How much it costs

![Cost of first localization: General Translation $1,000 to $1,500, human translators $90,000 to $265,000, on a scale from $0 to $300,000](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-cost.png?v=eb3ab1f3)

Professional translation runs [$0.10](https://translated.com/translation-rates) to [$0.30](https://www.smartling.com/blog/translation-rates) per word. At 850,000 words that is $85,000 to $255,000, or $5,000 to $15,000 per language; the figure in the chart also includes an estimate for the engineering work to connect your app to the translators. Using General Translation, the [Starter plan](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing) has no platform fee, so the cost is usage: 850,000 words is about 1.1 million tokens, [billed at $1 per 1,000 tokens](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing/usage). The same rates apply to every new word you ship after launch. In each chart, the solid part of a bar is the low end of the estimate and the faded part is the high end.

## How long it takes

![Time to first localized release: General Translation set up in under 10 minutes, human translators 25 to 35 business days](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-first-release.png?v=698d62d4)

With human translators, the engineering setup comes first; translation then runs at [2,000 to 2,500 words a day for each translator](https://www.pactranz.com/translation-times/). General Translation sets up in [under 10 minutes](https://generaltranslation.com/docs/react/nextjs-quickstart) in a Next.js or React app and translates everything on the first deploy. When you add new text later, human translators repeat the whole cycle; General Translation translates it instantly on the next deploy, and a new language is one line of config.

Check the math against your own app: pricing is published at [generaltranslation.com/pricing](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing).
