You have an app with 50,000 words of UI copy and you want it in many languages. What does year one cost, and how long until the first localized release? It depends on the pipeline.

This post compares the two pipelines teams choose between: a translation management system (TMS) with professional linguists, and General Translation. Every figure is an estimated range built from published rates, linked inline. Swap in your own numbers where yours differ.

## The scenario

- 50,000 words of source copy (a mid-sized SaaS app).
- 17 target locales: [CSA Research forecasts](https://www.mytotalretail.com/article/content-localization-as-the-key-to-success-in-global-markets/) that 17 languages will reach 90% of internet-accessible GDP by 2027. Market reach, not a per-company average.
- 850,000 words translated in year one.
- Engineer time at $700 to $900 a day. Estimate.

## TMS plus linguists

Your strings sync to a TMS such as Lokalise, Crowdin, or Phrase. Linguists translate them in batches, and finished translations ship with your next release.

Year-one costs:

- **Translation.** Published US rates run [$0.15 to $0.30 per word](https://www.smartling.com/blog/translation-rates); agencies advertise [$0.10 on average](https://translated.com/translation-rates) and [$0.10 to $0.22 for common pairs](https://thetranslationcompany.com/resources/5-facts-buying-translation/translation-pricing.htm). At 850,000 words: roughly $85,000 to $255,000, again every time your copy changes.
- **Platform.** Published tiers run [about $150](https://crowdin.com/pricing) to [about $1,250 a month](https://phrase.com/pricing/), with [Lokalise at $375](https://lokalise.com/pricing): call it $2,000 to $15,000 a year.
- **Setup.** One to two engineer-weeks wiring the sync, workflows, and review chain.

Agencies publish [2,000](https://www.betranslated.co.uk/translation-agency/turnaround/) to [2,500 words per day](https://www.pactranz.com/translation-times/) per linguist, so 50,000 words is four to five weeks per language unless you parallelize. A new locale is a new vendor engagement at the same rates.

## General Translation

Tag the strings in your code. AI models translate them on deploy, and a CDN serves every locale. There is no file export and no batch handoff.

Year-one costs:

- **Platform.** The [Starter plan](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing) has no platform fee.
- **Usage.** 850,000 words is about 1.1 million model tokens; at [$1 per 1,000 input tokens](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing/usage), about $1,000 to $1,500.
- **Integration.** Half a day to a day for a typical Next.js or React app.

New content translates on the next deploy. A new locale is one line of config.

## Assumptions

- One English word counts as 1.33 model tokens (OpenAI's rule of thumb).
- Ranges come from the vendor pages linked above, fetched August 2026. Unpublished tiers are left out.
- This compares cost and turnaround, not translation quality.

Check the math against your own app: pricing is published at [generaltranslation.com/pricing](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing).
