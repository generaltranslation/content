You have an app with 50,000 words of UI copy and you want to launch it in 17 languages: 850,000 words to translate in year one. [CSA Research forecasts](https://www.mytotalretail.com/article/content-localization-as-the-key-to-success-in-global-markets/) that 17 languages will reach 90% of internet-accessible GDP by 2027. Here is what that costs with a translation management system (TMS) and professional linguists, and what it costs using General Translation.

## Year-one cost

![Year-one cost: General Translation $1,000 to $2,500, TMS plus linguists $90,000 to $280,000, on a scale from $0 to $300,000](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-cost.png?v=41325c80)

Most of the TMS cost is the translation itself. Published US rates run [$0.15 to $0.30 per word](https://www.smartling.com/blog/translation-rates), and agencies advertise [$0.10 on average](https://translated.com/translation-rates) or [$0.10 to $0.22 for common language pairs](https://thetranslationcompany.com/resources/5-facts-buying-translation/translation-pricing.htm). The platform adds [about $150](https://crowdin.com/pricing) to [about $1,250 a month](https://phrase.com/pricing/). Using General Translation, the [Starter plan](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing) has no platform fee, so the cost is usage: 850,000 words is about 1.1 million tokens, [billed at $1 per 1,000](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing/usage). In each chart, the solid part of a bar is the low end of the estimate and the faded part is the high end.

## Time to first localized release

![Time to first localized release: General Translation half a day to 1 day, TMS plus linguists 25 to 35 business days](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-first-release.png?v=41d02c2f)

Agencies publish [2,000 to 2,500 words per linguist per day](https://www.pactranz.com/translation-times/), and the TMS pipeline takes a week or two to set up before translation starts. General Translation takes half a day to a day to integrate into a typical Next.js or React app, then translates everything on the first deploy.

## Turnaround for new content

![Turnaround for 50,000 new words: General Translation on the next deploy, TMS plus linguists 20 to 25 days per linguist](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-turnaround.png?v=0de4774b)

When you ship new copy, the TMS pipeline runs again: 50,000 new words is four to five weeks per linguist unless you parallelize across more of them, and every word is billed at the same per-word rate. Using General Translation, new content translates automatically on the next deploy, and a new locale is one line of config.

Check the math against your own app: pricing is published at [generaltranslation.com/pricing](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing).
