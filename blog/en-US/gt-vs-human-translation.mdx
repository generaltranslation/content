You have an app with 50,000 words of UI copy and 17 target languages: 850,000 words to translate in year one. [CSA Research forecasts](https://www.mytotalretail.com/article/content-localization-as-the-key-to-success-in-global-markets/) that 17 languages will reach 90% of internet-accessible GDP by 2027; that is market reach, not a per-company average. Below is that scenario priced two ways: a translation management system (TMS) with professional linguists, and General Translation.

Every figure is an estimated range built from published rates, fetched August 2026 and linked in each section. Engineer time is costed at $700 to $900 a day, and one English word counts as about 1.33 model tokens. This compares cost and turnaround, not translation quality.

## Year-one cost

![Year-one cost: General Translation $1,000 to $2,500, TMS plus linguists $90,000 to $280,000, on a scale from $0 to $300,000](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-cost.png?v=41325c80)

Human translation dominates the TMS bar: published US rates run [$0.15 to $0.30 per word](https://www.smartling.com/blog/translation-rates), agencies advertise [$0.10 on average](https://translated.com/translation-rates) and [$0.10 to $0.22 for common pairs](https://thetranslationcompany.com/resources/5-facts-buying-translation/translation-pricing.htm), and the platform adds [about $150](https://crowdin.com/pricing) to [about $1,250 a month](https://phrase.com/pricing/) plus one to two engineer-weeks of setup. The General Translation bar is usage and integration: the [Starter plan](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing) has no platform fee, and 850,000 words is about 1.1 million tokens at [$1 per 1,000](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing/usage). The solid stretch of each bar is the low end of the range; the faded stretch runs to the high end.

## Time to first localized release

![Time to first localized release: General Translation half a day to 1 day, TMS plus linguists 25 to 35 business days](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-first-release.png?v=41d02c2f)

Agencies publish [2,000](https://www.betranslated.co.uk/translation-agency/turnaround/) to [2,500 words per day](https://www.pactranz.com/translation-times/) per linguist, after one to two engineer-weeks wiring the sync, workflows, and review chain. General Translation integrates in half a day to a day for a typical Next.js or React app and translates on the first deploy.

## Turnaround for new content

![Turnaround for 50,000 new words: General Translation on the next deploy, TMS plus linguists 20 to 25 days per linguist](/static/blogs/gt-vs-human-turnaround.png?v=0de4774b)

Changed copy pays the per-word rate again and re-enters the batch queue; 50,000 new words is four to five weeks per linguist unless you parallelize, and a new locale is a new vendor engagement. With General Translation, new content translates automatically on the next deploy, and a new locale is one line of config.

Check the math against your own app: pricing is published at [generaltranslation.com/pricing](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing).
