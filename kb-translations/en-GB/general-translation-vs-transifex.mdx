General Translation is the better fit when you want translation to follow your React development workflow: mark JSX with [`<T>`](/docs/react/reference/components/t), generate translations before the build and ship them with the app. Transifex is a translation management system (TMS) that combines AI, translators and partner vendors, and has been [part of XTM International](https://xtm.ai/news/xtm-international-acquires-transifex) since January 2025.

Transifex supports both resource files and strings marked directly in code through Transifex Native. Native pushes messages to Transifex and loads translations from its content delivery service (CDS). General Translation extracts marked JSX for translation and lets the app load the result from bundled files or its CDN.

## What Transifex does well

* **Strings from code without files.** [Transifex Native](https://developers.transifex.com/docs/javascript-sdk) marks strings with a translation function or a React component, uses the source text as the key by default, and pushes strings with `txjs-cli push`. The SDKs and the [CDS](https://github.com/transifex/transifex-delivery) are open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, and the CDS can be self-hosted.
* **AI with quality scores.** Transifex AI translates using your glossary, translation memory and context, and its [Translation Quality Index (TQI)](https://help.transifex.com/en/articles/9465000-translation-quality-index-tqi) scores AI translations. Those that meet a configured threshold can be marked as reviewed automatically.
* **Human translation partners.** You can [order translations from partners](https://help.transifex.com/en/articles/6235673-ordering-translations) such as e2f, Gengo and TextMaster from within Transifex.
* **Websites without code changes.** [Transifex Live](https://help.transifex.com/en/articles/6244596-introduction-to-transifex-live) translates a website via a JavaScript snippet, although Transifex recommends Native for React and Vue apps.
* **Unlimited collaborators.** The current plan doesn&#39;t charge for seats, projects or hosted content.
* **File formats.** [46+ formats](https://help.transifex.com/en/articles/6219670-introduction-to-file-formats), including XLIFF, Office files and subtitles.

## What Transifex still leaves to your engineers

Engineers connect the app to Transifex&#39;s translation and delivery workflow:

1. Mark each string for Transifex: extract it into a resource file under a key, or wrap it in Native&#39;s translation function or React component.
2. Send it to Transifex with [`tx push`](https://github.com/transifex/cli), a Git integration, or `txjs-cli push`, typically as a CI step.
3. Configure AI translation or a partner vendor, along with any review requirements.
4. Deliver the translations. In the file workflow, a [Git integration](https://help.transifex.com/en/articles/6265125-github-via-transifex-ui) opens a pull request or commits once a language reaches a completion threshold. Native fetches translations from the [CDS](https://developers.transifex.com/docs/content-delivery-service), whose content becomes stale after one hour by default, although the refresh interval is configurable.

Transifex AI uses [glossary terms, translation memory, context fields, and company information](https://help.transifex.com/en/articles/9867663-context-in-transifex-ai). Developer notes and string instructions can also be enabled. General Translation&#39;s React library supplies context through the marked JSX structure and named placeholders, with developer notes and project terminology added to guide translation.

Pricing consists of a single AI-first plan sold through the sales team and billed on AI-translated words, with no public rates. (See [Transifex pricing](https://www.transifex.com/pricing), checked October 2026.)

## Why teams choose General Translation over Transifex

* **Faster.** [`npx gt translate`](/docs/cli/reference/commands/translate) extracts marked content, generates translations and downloads the results before the app build. That gives a release a single translation step to run. If you prefer reviewing changes in GitHub, [Locadex](/docs/platform/locadex/quickstart) opens a pull request when your code changes.
* **Cheaper to run.** General Translation reduces the engineering work involved in translating rich UI: a sentence can keep its links, formatting and dynamic values inside one [`<T>`](/docs/react/reference/components/t) block rather than being reconstructed as a message string. You can estimate translation costs directly from the [published rates](https://generaltranslation.com/pricing).
* **Higher quality.** Keeping a sentence and its nested elements together preserves the relationship between the words, links and placeholders during translation. Developer notes clarify ambiguous copy, while assigned [Context Groups](/docs/platform/dashboard/guides/defining-context-for-translations) apply the same terminology across your app and website. Your team can refine the output in the [Translation Editor](/docs/platform/dashboard/guides/reviewing-translations).
* **Lower risk.** With [local translations](/docs/react/guides/storing-translations), the app renders from files bundled in the release and makes no runtime calls to General Translation. Teams that want to publish translation edits without redeploying can use General Translation&#39;s CDN instead. The MIT-licensed JavaScript libraries work without an account when you supply the translations.

Sierra ships 7+ locales across its web and go-to-market content with General Translation. (See [production deployments](https://generaltranslation.com/enterprise).)

## When Transifex is the better fit

Choose Transifex when you want to translate a website with a snippet and no code changes, when you want to order human translation from its partner vendors, when TQI-based auto-approval fits your review process, when you exchange XLIFF, Office or subtitle files, or when native iOS and Android apps need [over-the-air updates with offline bundles](https://developers.transifex.com/docs/ios-sdk).

## Moving from Transifex to General Translation

For file-based projects using supported formats such as JSON, YAML, PO, or iOS and Android resource files, follow the [CLI quickstart](/docs/cli/quickstart). Configure source and locale paths, then import existing translations with [`npx gt upload`](/docs/cli/reference/commands/upload) before generating new ones.

For a React app using Native, set up `gt-react` or `gt-next` through the [React quickstart](/docs/react/react-quickstart) or [Next.js quickstart](/docs/react/nextjs-quickstart). Migrate one screen at a time: put its copy and markup inside [`<T>`](/docs/react/reference/components/t), then run [`npx gt translate`](/docs/cli/reference/commands/translate). Keep the remaining screens on Native while you review the translated screen. Import your glossary into a [Context Group](/docs/platform/dashboard/guides/defining-context-for-translations) to carry your product terminology over.

## Is General Translation an alternative to Transifex?

Yes. Choose General Translation to generate translations from structured JSX before your build and deliver them as bundled files or through its CDN. You can also keep an existing file-based app and use General Translation to translate its catalogues. For a broader vendor comparison, see [Why use General Translation instead of a TMS?](/kb/general-translation-vs-tms).

## How is Transifex Native different from General Translation?

Both use open-source SDKs and can keep source text in code. In [Transifex&#39;s React SDK](https://developers.transifex.com/docs/react-sdk), a `<T _str="...">` message can receive React elements as interpolation values. General Translation&#39;s [`<T>`](/docs/react/reference/components/t) keeps the sentence and nested elements directly in JSX and extracts that structure for translation. Native delivers translations through the CDS; General Translation lets you choose between bundled files and its CDN.

## Key takeaways

* Transifex is a TMS with open-source Native SDKs, AI translation with quality scores, partner vendors, and Transifex Live for websites.
* Native already supports strings in code. General Translation&#39;s React advantage is authoring and translating structured JSX, with developer notes and shared terminology.
* General Translation generates translations before the build and supports both bundled delivery and CDN updates, so you can choose how translated content reaches users.
