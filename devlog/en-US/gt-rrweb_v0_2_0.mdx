## Overview

`gt-rrweb` records a product walkthrough once with rrweb, then replays it in each locale using your app's published translations. `GTRecorder` and `useRecorder` capture the session, `gt-rrweb/harvest` assembles the locale data, and `createGTReplayer` or `GTReplayer` handles playback. The replayer supports live locale switching, scrubbing, theme controls, full-screen playback, and a cursor generated from recorded clicks. This early 0.x release is intended for localized demos and product tours.
