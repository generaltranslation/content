## Overview

`gt-rrweb` records a product walkthrough once with rrweb, then replays it in each locale using your app's published translations. [`GTRecorder`](/docs/integrations/rrweb/reference/recorder#gt-recorder) and [`useRecorder`](/docs/integrations/rrweb/reference/recorder#use-recorder) capture the session, `gt-rrweb/harvest` assembles the locale data, and [`createGTReplayer`](/docs/integrations/rrweb/reference/replayer#create-gt-replayer) or [`GTReplayer`](/docs/integrations/rrweb/reference/replayer#gt-replayer) handles playback. The replayer supports live locale switching, scrubbing, theme controls, full-screen playback, and a cursor generated from recorded clicks. This early 0.x release is intended for localized demos and product tours.
