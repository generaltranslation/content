## Overview

`gt-node` v2 picks up the breaking changes of [`gt-i18n` 2](/devlog/gt-i18n_v2_0_0) and [`generaltranslation` 10](/devlog/generaltranslation_v10) through its re-exported types and [`initializeGT()`](/docs/node/reference/functions/initialize-gt) options.

## Breaking changes

- **The re-exported `GTConfig` is narrower.** It drops `devApiKey`, `files.gt.includeSourceCodeContext` (use `files.gt.parsingFlags.includeSourceCodeContext`), and `parsingFlags.legacyGtReactImportSource`. The [`devApiKey`](/docs/node/reference/config#dev-api-key) option of [`initializeGT()`](/docs/node/reference/functions/initialize-gt) is unchanged.
- **[`runtimeTranslation`](/docs/node/reference/config#runtime-translation) `metadata` accepts only `sourceLocale` and `modelProvider`.** Other keys are rejected by the type.
- **[`runtimeTranslation`](/docs/node/reference/config#runtime-translation) `timeout: 0` is a zero timeout.** Omit `timeout` to use the default.
- **`RuntimeTranslationOptions` drops `$_source`.**

## Upgrading

1. Remove keys other than `sourceLocale` and `modelProvider` from `runtimeTranslation.metadata`.
2. Replace `runtimeTranslation.timeout: 0` with no `timeout` if you relied on the default.
3. Remove `$_source` from runtime translation options.
